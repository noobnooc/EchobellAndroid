Echobell turns webhooks, channel events, and automation signals into instant Android alerts.

Send a webhook, create a channel, or use a direct key to notify your phone when something needs attention. Echobell is built for developers, operators, makers, traders, and teams who need practical alerts from the systems they already use.

Use Echobell for:

- Production incidents, API errors, outages, and service maintenance
- Server, uptime, cron, backup, and infrastructure monitoring
- CI/CD pipelines, deploy status, build failures, and release events
- Stock market alerts, trading signals, strategy scripts, and market automation
- Payment, order, customer, and business workflow updates
- Security events, access alerts, suspicious activity, and audit signals
- Smart home sensors, personal scripts, scheduled jobs, and custom automations

Why Echobell works well for critical events:

- Webhook and channel-based alerts for flexible integrations
- Direct keys for simple one-off webhook triggers
- Standard, time-sensitive, and call-style alert modes
- Custom notification templates for titles, bodies, links, notes, and conditions
- Notification records stored on this device for quick review
- External links that take you from an alert to the right dashboard, chart, runbook, or incident page
- Channel sharing and subscription controls for team and multi-device workflows
- Premium support for unlimited channels, direct keys, and alert modes

Echobell does not replace your monitoring stack, trading tools, automation platform, or incident system. It gives them a fast mobile alert layer, so the events that matter can reach the phone you actually carry.

From API incidents and deploy failures to market signals, backup jobs, security alerts, and business events, Echobell helps critical events reach you quickly and stay easy to review.
