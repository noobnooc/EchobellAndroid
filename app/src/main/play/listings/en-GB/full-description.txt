Echobell turns webhooks and emails into instant push notifications on your phone, and critical alerts can ring like a phone call so an outage never sits unread in the notification shade.

Send one HTTP request and your phone knows. Echobell is built for developers, SREs, indie makers, home lab owners, traders and small teams who need real alerts from the systems they already run.

THREE ALERT LEVELS
• Standard — an ordinary push notification for routine events
• Time-Sensitive — cuts through so it gets noticed sooner
• Call — rings your phone like an incoming call for critical alerts

CHANNELS AND DIRECT KEYS
• Channels group related alerts, carry templates and conditions, and can be shared so a whole team subscribes from one link
• Direct keys are single-purpose webhook URLs for a script, a cron job or a quick integration
• Trigger a channel with a webhook request or by sending an email to its address

MAKE EVERY ALERT READABLE
• Title and body templates turn a raw JSON payload into a sentence you can act on
• Conditions filter noise, so only events that matter become notifications
• Link templates open the right dashboard, chart, runbook or incident page straight from the alert
• Notification records stay on this device for quick review

WHAT PEOPLE USE ECHOBELL FOR
• Server monitoring, uptime checks, downtime and recovery alerts
• Production incidents, API errors, on-call paging and status changes
• CI/CD pipelines, deploy notifications, build failures and release events
• Cron jobs, backup jobs, scheduled tasks and heartbeat monitoring
• Home automation, self-hosted services, NAS and home lab sensors
• Stock and crypto price alerts, trading signals and strategy scripts
• Payment, order and customer events for small businesses
• Security events, login alerts and audit signals

WORKS WITH WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
Anything that can send an HTTP request can send an Echobell notification: shell scripts, Python, Node, automation platforms, monitoring stacks, uptime checkers, CI runners, home automation hubs and trading bots. No SDK to install, no agent to run.

PREMIUM
Echobell Premium unlocks unlimited channels, unlimited direct keys and call-style alerts. Invite points can also be redeemed for premium access.

Echobell does not replace your monitoring stack, automation platform or incident tool. It gives them a fast mobile alert layer, so the events that matter reach the phone you actually carry.
